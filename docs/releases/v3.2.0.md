# Vocello 3.2.0

Released **November 2026**. Vocello 3.2 makes the local engine leaner and steadier, fixes live
streaming preview progress so the playhead counter and waveform bar advance smoothly while a clip
is still generating, hardens long-form and command-line reliability, and brings clearer AI
transparency and safeguards to Settings. Everything still runs locally on Apple Silicon; no audio
leaves your Mac unless you export it. The iPhone app's 3.2 version, on the same engine and also
admitting eligible 8 GB+ iPads in portrait compatibility mode, follows through TestFlight and the
App Store.

> **Upgrading from 3.1?** Choose **Vocello > Check for Updates**, or install the new DMG as usual.
> Your History, saved voices, settings and installed models carry over with **no model
> re-download**, and seeds pinned in 3.1 reproduce their takes in 3.2.
>
> **Upgrading from 3.0?** Choose **Vocello > Check for Updates**, or install the new DMG as usual.
> Your History, saved voices and settings carry over. On first launch Vocello replaces the models
> you installed with 3.0 by the one model for each mode, on its own on an unmetered network (a
> banner shows the progress, and **Not Now** stops it). Your earlier models are removed once a new
> one is ready, and Vocello tells you how much space that freed.
>
> **Upgrading from 2.x?** Install the new DMG by hand once: 2.x has no updater. Your History, saved
> voices and settings carry over, and the models you had installed are replaced the same way. If a
> mode reads Not installed afterwards, install its model from Settings > Models & Files.

## What's new

- **A leaner, steadier engine.** The code predictor's compiled execution plan is hoisted and
  prewarmed with weights passed as compile inputs so cross-thread MLX compile caches are not
  retained between takes, talker repetition penalties use a fixed-shape GPU seen-mask, per-step
  introspection is gated behind diagnostics, Mimi encoder KV retention and causal-mask handling are
  fixed, and quantization and fusion invariants fail closed. Audio samples are identical to 3.1 on
  the same seeds; on the benchmark Mac mini M6, warm median real-time factor moves from 0.315 to
  0.308 (about 18 seconds to make one minute of speech, down from 19 seconds) with lower warm
  retained memory.
- **Live streaming preview tracks playback smoothly.** Fixed a timer-starvation bug where incoming
  audio chunks reset the live preview's 3 Hz playhead timer, leaving elapsed time stuck at `0:00`
  and the waveform bar immobile while a clip was still generating. The elapsed time counter and
  waveform bar now advance smoothly throughout generation in both Studio and the sidebar player,
  hold monotonic progress across arriving chunks and the live-to-final handoff, and scrub back
  immediately once the take finishes.
- **Long-form, cloning and storage reliability.** Unspeakable or punctuation-only paragraphs in
  long-form scripts are folded cleanly instead of stalling a segment, scripts containing only
  control tokens are rejected before generation starts, Korean long-form cautions surface clearly
  in Studio and the command line, model downloads and History outbox writes are hardened against
  partial or corrupted state, and an unwritable output folder reports a storage failure instead of
  an engine fault.
- **Clearer transparency and AI safeguards.** Mac Settings > Voice Cloning now includes an
  AudioSeal watermarking disclosure alongside the jurisdiction-neutral cloning consent note;
  Settings > Updates links directly to Terms of Use and Report an Issue; voice-imitation guardrails
  are localized across all ten interface languages; and Meta AudioSeal (MIT) is listed in Open
  Source & Licenses.
- **Same models, no re-download from 3.1.** Pre-registered automated screens evaluated candidate
  mixed-precision alternatives against the 3.1 models and did not clear the promotion bar, so
  Vocello 3.2 keeps the single model per mode introduced in 3.1 unchanged. Upgrading from 3.1
  downloads only the app and CLI package.

## What's new: the `vocello` command line

- **Hardened lifecycle and 8 GB Mac coordination.** Forced exits use `_exit` so teardown hooks do
  not contend with an active engine, 8 GB single-engine host leases and model-deletion gates are
  hardened, and benchmark telemetry records the hybrid quantization tier directly (`hybrid`).
- **Clearer preflight and storage errors.** Long-form scripts sent as Korean print
  `long_form_korean` before any model work, and an unwritable output folder reports a storage
  failure instead of an engine fault.
- **Same models and compatible contract.** Model ids (`pro_custom_speed`, `pro_design_speed`,
  `pro_clone_speed`) and installed weights are unchanged from 3.1, so updating from 3.1 requires no
  model re-download.

## Requirements

- macOS 26.0 or later, Apple Silicon, and at least 8 GB unified memory (16 GB or more recommended
  for long projects).
- An administrator password to install the CLI package.
- Internet access for the one-time model downloads from Hugging Face; model files are not bundled.
  Each mode downloads about 2.2 GB on its own (updating from 3.1 needs no model download; updating
  all three modes from 3.0 downloads about 4.7 GB, because the shared speech tokenizer is reused).
  Check the displayed sizes and available storage first. Generation runs locally once a model is
  installed.
- Microphone access for recording, and on-device Speech Recognition for automatic transcription.
  Manual transcript review stays available when recognition cannot finish.
- iPhone and iPad: iOS 26.0 or later on iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an eligible Apple silicon or A17
  Pro iPad with 8 GB+ memory running in portrait compatibility mode.

## Install

Download from the [v3.2.0 release](https://github.com/PowerBeef/Vocello/releases/tag/v3.2.0).

1. **Mac app:** from 3.1 or 3.0, choose **Vocello > Check for Updates**. Otherwise open
   `Vocello-macos26.dmg` and drag `Vocello.app` to Applications.
2. **Command line:** open `Vocello-macos26-cli.pkg` and follow the installer, then run
   `vocello --help`. Or open `Vocello-macos26-cli.dmg` and copy the complete **Vocello CLI** folder
   to a folder you own, keeping its executable and resource bundles together.
3. **AI agents:** with Claude Code, run `claude mcp add --scope user vocello -- /usr/local/bin/vocello mcp`.
4. Coming from 3.1, your installed models are already ready. Coming from 3.0 or a fresh install,
   open Settings > Models & Files (or run `vocello models install`) so each mode's model is ready.
   Back up important exported audio and saved references before upgrading.

stapled, attested, and checked again after upload; the CLI package is installed, upgraded and

Known limitations:

- Each mode's model (introduced in 3.1 and kept unchanged in 3.2) clones a voice about as closely
  as the 3.0 Speed model, not as closely as 3.0 Quality, and its long-form error rate sits between
  the two.
- Korean long-form projects still stop on about half the scripts in automated screens; when a
  segment is refused, Resume continues with a new take of it.
- Rarely, a take says its text and then runs on in silence. Vocello stops it after three seconds and
  the audio check refuses it (under about 1.5% in screened takes). Generate again.
- The audio check can still refuse a take in any language, and Vocello rejects such a take instead
  of saving it. Generate again: Vocello never silently regenerates, substitutes or accepts a failed
  take.
- Takes made with the `vocello` command line are saved as files and still do not appear in History;
  a later update adds that.
- A seed pinned with 3.1 reproduces its take in 3.2. A seed pinned with 3.0 or earlier does not
  reproduce its old take, because the model changed in 3.1.

Take quality in 3.2 is not verified by human listening. Automated checks catch gross failures
(silence, a cut-off or unfinished take, missing words, the wrong language), but a take can still
carry subtle defects: a swallowed syllable, a short false start, an accent from another language, or
delivery that misses the requested style. Generate again for a different take: Vocello never
silently regenerates, substitutes or accepts a failed take.
