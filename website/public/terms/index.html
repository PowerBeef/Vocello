Audio watermarking and AI disclosure

Every generated WAV take carries an inaudible AudioSeal watermark with a fixed, non-identifying Vocello payload and WAV LIST/INFO provenance metadata marking it as AI-generated. Reference clips you record or import and live preview audio heard before saving are not marked.

  • Do not remove, alter, or bypass the watermark or provenance metadata to misrepresent synthetic speech as genuine human audio.
  • When you publish or distribute audio made with Vocello, particularly audio of a cloned real voice, disclose that it is AI-generated wherever applicable laws or platform policies require it.
  • Open-source AudioSeal tools can inspect exported WAV files for the Vocello watermark, though external transcoding, heavy compression, or re-encoding after export may degrade watermark detectability.

Voice models are not bundled inside the app. When you choose to install a model, Vocello downloads immutable model weight files directly from Hugging Face and verifies their cryptographic SHA-256 digests locally before activation.
