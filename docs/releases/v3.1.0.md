# Vocello 3.1.0

Released **November 2026**. Vocello 3.1 replaces each mode's two models, Speed and Quality, with one
improved model, and updates the models you already have on its own. Voice cloning follows its
reference more faithfully, every take samples the way the model was designed to, and the engine is
leaner. Everything still runs locally on Apple Silicon; no audio leaves your Mac unless you export it.
The iPhone app's 3.1 version, on the same engine, follows through TestFlight and the App Store.

> **Upgrading from 3.0?** Choose **Vocello > Check for Updates**, or install the new DMG as usual.
> Your History, saved voices and settings carry over. On first launch Vocello replaces the models
> you installed with 3.0 by the new one for each mode, on its own on an unmetered network (a banner
> shows the progress, and **Not Now** stops it). Your earlier models are removed once a new one is
> ready, and Vocello tells you how much space that freed.
>
> **Upgrading from 2.x?** Install the new DMG by hand once: 2.x has no updater. Your History, saved
> voices and settings carry over, and the models you had installed are replaced the same way. If a
> mode reads Not installed afterwards, install its model from Settings → Models & Files.

## What's new

- **One improved model per mode.** Built-in Voice, Voice Design and Voice Cloning each use a single
  model that keeps the 4-bit model's size and puts its attention layers at 8 bits (about 180 MB
  more). In Vocello's automated screens it makes fewer mistakes than the old Speed model on single
  takes and long projects, finishes more long projects, and clones a voice as closely as Speed did;
  it takes about 9% more time than the old Speed model and about 27% less than Quality. The
  Speed/Quality switch and the "Prefer lower-memory models" setting are gone, and each mode's package
  row simply reads Model.
- **Your models update themselves.** After an update from 3.0, the modes whose models you installed
  read **Update required** and update on their own on an unmetered network, one or two at a time; a
  mode you never installed stays a first install. A banner above every page shows the progress,
  offers **Update Now** or **Not Now**, and ends with the space your earlier models freed. Each mode
  downloads about 2.2 GB on its own; updating all three downloads about 4.7 GB, because the speech
  tokenizer they share with your earlier models is reused. On a disk too full to keep the earlier
  models meanwhile, Vocello offers to remove them first, and says so on the button. A cancelled or
  failed update waits for you to choose Update; Vocello never restarts it on its own.
- **Voice cloning follows its reference more faithfully.** A reference clip is prepared the same way
  every time, a stereo clip is mixed down as the reference implementation does, and silent edges are
  trimmed. A cloned take that plays while it is generated now hears the reference's own speech first,
  which in Vocello's screens brought it measurably closer to the reference voice. Chinese and
  Japanese targets join the reference transcript as the reference implementation joins them.
- **Clearer feedback on references.** Saving a voice checks the original recording and names what
  is wrong with it (a low sample rate, near-silence or clipping). A recognized transcript that does
  not cover the whole clip waits for your review instead of saving as Ready, enrollment asks the
  recognizer for punctuation, and **Use audio only** on a saved voice is never overridden by its
  stored transcript.
- **One way to sample a take.** The Variation setting is retired: every take samples as the model's
  reference implementation does (the former Expressive). A saved Balanced or Consistent preference
  reads as Expressive, and History still shows an older take's recorded variation. In the long-form
  screens Consistent stopped far more projects than Expressive and showed no benefit.
- **A caution for Korean long-form scripts.** Korean long-form projects stop more often than other
  languages, so Studio shows a caution under a script that Auto sends as Korean, before you start.
- **A leaner, quicker engine.** About 400 fewer GPU kernel launches per frame and the text embedding
  read from the model file by row (about 315 MB less resident memory), both with identical samples;
  the live preview starts sooner; the watermark pass finishes faster, and every long-form segment is
  joined on the watermark's frame so the joined file reads as marked.
- **Stricter automatic checks.** A take far too short for its script now fails Vocello's audio check,
  silence is judged before the watermark is applied, and a take that runs on in silence stops after
  three seconds instead of filling its length limit.

## What's new: the `vocello` command line

- **The same models and update.** `vocello models list` marks a mode whose 3.0 model needs its
  update, and `vocello models install` installs the new model and removes the earlier folders. The
  model ids are unchanged (`pro_custom_speed`, `pro_design_speed`, `pro_clone_speed`).
- **Retired options still parse.** `--variant quality`, `models use <mode> quality` and `models
  prefer-lower-memory` print a `variant_retired` warning and use the mode's one model;
  `--variation balanced|consistent` prints `variation_retired` and generates as Expressive. Scripts
  written for 3.0 keep running.
- **A Korean long-form warning.** A long-form script sent as Korean prints `long_form_korean` before
  any model work.

## Requirements

- macOS 26.0 or later, Apple Silicon, and at least 8 GB unified memory (16 GB or more recommended
  for long projects).
- An administrator password to install the CLI package.
- Internet access for the one-time model downloads from Hugging Face; model files are not bundled.
  Each mode downloads about 2.2 GB on its own, and updating all three from 3.0 downloads about
  4.7 GB, because the speech tokenizer they share is reused. Check the displayed sizes and available
  storage first. Generation runs locally once a model is installed.
- Microphone access for recording, and on-device Speech Recognition for automatic transcription.
  Manual transcript review stays available when recognition cannot finish.
- iPhone: iOS 26.0 or later on iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

## Install

Download from the [v3.1.0 release](https://github.com/PowerBeef/Vocello/releases/tag/v3.1.0).

1. **Mac app:** from 3.0, choose **Vocello > Check for Updates**. Otherwise open
   `Vocello-macos26.dmg` and drag `Vocello.app` to Applications.
2. **Command line:** open `Vocello-macos26-cli.pkg` and follow the installer, then run
   `vocello --help`. Or open `Vocello-macos26-cli.dmg` and copy the complete **Vocello CLI** folder
   to a folder you own, keeping its executable and resource bundles together.
3. **AI agents:** with Claude Code, run `claude mcp add --scope user vocello -- /usr/local/bin/vocello mcp`.
4. Let the model update finish (Settings → Models & Files shows each mode), or install a mode's model
   there or with `vocello models install`. Back up important exported audio and saved references
   before upgrading.

## Evidence and limitations

Every 3.1 file is built by the release workflow from the tagged commit, only after CI has passed on
that exact commit. Both DMGs and the CLI package are signed with Developer ID, notarized and
stapled, attested, and checked again after upload; the CLI package is installed, upgraded and
uninstalled on a clean machine before release.

The new model was chosen by machine screens whose decision rules were recorded before their takes
were generated: 192 clone takes in six languages, 72 long-form projects and 144 single takes,
against both 3.0 models. On the benchmark Mac mini M6, warm, its median real-time factor (seconds of
generation per second of audio, lower is faster) is 0.30, against 0.28 for the 3.0 Speed model and
0.41 for Quality, measured in alternation on one build: about 9% more time than Speed and about
27% less than Quality, from the unrounded medians (0.303, 0.279 and 0.412). The model update was proven on a copy of a
real 3.0 Mac library: about 4.7 GB downloaded for three modes, History and saved voices intact, and
a passing take in every mode. These measurements were made on builds compiled with Xcode 27.2; the
released app and command line are compiled from the same source with Xcode 26.6, so the release's
own numbers may differ slightly.

Known limitations:

- The new model clones a voice about as closely as the old Speed model, not as closely as Quality,
  and its long-form error rate sits between the two.
- Korean long-form projects still stop on about half the scripts in the screens; when a segment is
  refused, Resume continues with a new take of it.
- Rarely, a take says its text and then runs on in silence. Vocello stops it after three seconds and
  the audio check refuses it. Outside one fixed benchmark seed, none of 204 screened takes did this,
  which puts the rate under about 1.5%. Generate again.
- The audio check can still refuse a take in any language, and Vocello rejects such a take instead
  of saving it. Generate again: Vocello never silently regenerates, substitutes or accepts a failed
  take.
- Takes made with the `vocello` command line are saved as files and still do not appear in History;
  a later update adds that.
- A seed pinned with 3.0 no longer reproduces its take, because the model changed.

The 3.0 note about long-form projects stalling with the Consistent variation no longer applies:
the Variation setting is retired and every take samples as Expressive.

Take quality in 3.1 is not verified by human listening. Automated checks catch gross failures
(silence, a cut-off or unfinished take, missing words, the wrong language), but a take can still
carry subtle defects: a swallowed syllable, a short false start, an accent from another language, or
delivery that misses the requested style. Generate again for a different take: Vocello never
silently regenerates, substitutes or accepts a failed take.

## TestFlight: what to test

The iPhone app's 3.1 follows the Mac release. It has no TestFlight candidate yet, so there is no
What to Test text to paste into App Store Connect. That text is added to this section with the first
iPhone 3.1 candidate, on the same engine and model as the Mac app's 3.1.0.

[support page](https://vocello.vercel.app/support/), and leave private reference audio and
transcripts out.
